About me

Hi

Curious beginner, careful builder — I'm Dedeh, a student from Nairobi learning to code.

I fell for the web after building my first (very ugly) HTML page, and since then I've been practising every week — learning how layouts hold together, why accessibility matters, and how to write CSS that behaves on every screen size.

I like clear content, calm design, and the small details that make a page feel considered. Right now I'm looking for an internship or junior role where I can keep learning from people who've been doing this longer than me.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me sketching interfaces, reading about type, or taking long walks around Nairobi with a coffee in hand.
